The big idea: guess the next word

Imagine texting and your phone suggests the next word.

  • "I'm going to the ___" → store, gym, beach
  • The phone is guessing what comes next

That's what a Transformer does — just astonishingly well.

2 / 17

Words become numbers

Computers don't understand words, only numbers.

  1. Split text into tokens (word pieces)
  2. Give each token an ID number
  3. Look up numbers that capture its meaning — an embedding

"cat" and "kitten" end up with similar numbers.

5 / 17

Attention = deciding what matters

Read: "The animal didn't cross the street because **it* was too tired."*

What does "it" mean — the animal, or the street?

Your brain instantly links it → animal. Attention lets the model do the same: every word looks at every other word and decides who matters.

The maths, gently

Don't panic — read it as a sentence:

  • Q — what am I looking for?
  • K — what does each word offer?

8 / 17

Many heads are better

One attention head notices one kind of relationship.

Multi-head attention runs many at once — grammar, meaning, position — then combines what they found. Like reading a sentence for tone and facts and structure at the same time.

The Transformer block

10 / 17

Two jobs per block

Attention lets words talk to each other.

The feed-forward layer lets each word think on its own.

Stack those two, add a little normalisation, and you have a Transformer block. Stack dozens and you have a language model.

11 / 17

How it learns

The model guesses the next word, checks the real answer, and nudges millions of numbers a tiny bit.

Repeat billions of times on huge amounts of text.

That's training. No magic — just a lot of small corrections.

12 / 17

Why it changed everything

  • Sees all words at once → fast on GPUs
  • Gets better smoothly with more data and compute
  • The same recipe works for text, code, images and audio
13 / 17

In code

import torch

def attention(q, k, v):
    scores = q @ k.transpose(-2, -1) / q.size(-1) ** 0.5
    weights = scores.softmax(dim=-1)
    return weights @ v

Six lines. Everything else is scale.
