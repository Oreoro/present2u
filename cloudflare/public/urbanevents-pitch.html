The problem

Event ticketing in Pakistan still runs on paper and cash: queue at the gate, fake tickets, no data. Organizers juggle bank transfers and spreadsheets; attendees have no trusted way to buy ahead.

Urban Events2 / 11

The solution

A self-serve platform: publish a branded event page, sell tickets in PKR through JazzCash, Easypaisa and bank transfer, and scan QR codes at the gate from any phone. Free for free events.
